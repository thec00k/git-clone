Overview

This is a breadth-first 2.5D working prototype of Keepsake, built across the Design Bible v3 phases (A–F). The goal is a coherent, testable experience that touches as many systems as possible rather than finishing any one to production depth. Everything runs in the browser (React + TypeScript + Vite, persisted to IndexedDB); there is no backend yet, so account, storage, and multi-user systems are simulated locally and labelled as such.

Art direction. The room and chrome draw on Little Big Planet (handcrafted, tactile, "pop-out" objects), Sly Cooper (storybook, bold stylised shapes, warm noir), and What Remains of Edith Finch (a cozy, slightly melancholic diorama house, warm light, drifting dust). It is realised in 2.5D with layered parallax, painterly gradients, grain, and soft vignette — the "2.5D over full 3D" open decision from Bible §4/§8.

Two conventions from your brief: ideas that deviate from the Bible are collected in the companion "Suggestions & Deviations" PDF; quick, obvious fixes and any clear privacy/security issues are acted on directly and noted here.
