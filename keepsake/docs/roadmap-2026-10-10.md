# Keepsake roadmap, October 10, 2026

Written after the foundations, seasons, hearth, stamps, lettering, holiday and ageing work on `codex/foundations-and-seasons`. Dates assume one developer working with Claude, and every date is a plan, not a promise. Prices and limits change: check each vendor's pricing page before committing.

## Where the project stands
**Works today (local only):** the Woodland room with four seasons and five holidays, hearth fire and sound, scrapbooks with Y2K and magazine-cutout lettering, photo archive and review stack, map, shelf, display case, daily stamps, free room swapping, cosmetic dust, backup/restore, CI.
**Does not exist yet:** accounts, any server, sharing/visiting/notes between people, moderation, real age/safety controls, server-side photo limits, analytics, a deployed site, the real window scenery, Beachfront/Neon/Sky Castle at the new art-direction standard.
**Biggest risk:** everything social, and every safety promise made for teens, depends on a backend that is not started. Dec 22 can only be a *private beta*, not a public launch.

## Next steps, in order
1. **Decisions:** backend vendor; teens and "open" rooms (recommend no); music policy; which holidays by region; who reviews reports (you? a part-time moderator?).
2. **Staging deploy** on Cloudflare Pages with the existing `_headers`; turn the CSP from report-only to enforcing once Spotify/SoundCloud are tested.
3. **Backend schema and auth** (see links). Keep the app local-first: the IndexedDB room stays the working copy and syncs up, so offline use and the existing backup/restore keep working.
4. **Move photo bytes out of the state object** (storage.ts keeps `modelSrc`, `coverSrc`, `backSrc` inline today; the audit flagged this).
5. **Safety stack** before any user can see another user's content: moderation, hash matching, reporting, blocking, rate limits.
6. **Art:** brief and commission (docs/scenery-brief.md); holiday art replaces the placeholder paper-craft shapes.
7. **Beachfront pass**, then the other rooms.
8. **Quality:** adapt the remaining browser checks to CI, add Git LFS for large assets, add a formatter, test on a real mid-range laptop and a real GPU.

## Services and places to look
**Hosting and backend**
- Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Row Level Security, Realtime): https://supabase.com/docs , pricing https://supabase.com/pricing , RLS guide https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security
- Cloudflare Pages (static hosting, headers, previews): https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/
- Cloudflare R2 (photo storage with no egress fees; compare against Supabase Storage): https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/ , pricing https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/
- Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection on sign-up): https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/
- Alternatives if you change your mind: Firebase https://firebase.google.com/docs , Appwrite https://appwrite.io/docs , Convex https://docs.convex.dev , Neon (Postgres) https://neon.com/docs

## Money, roughly (verify current prices)
Early on this can run on free tiers (Cloudflare Pages, Supabase free, Plausible trial) for staging. Expect real costs once you have users: Supabase Pro plan, R2 storage, a moderation API, email, analytics, a domain, commissioned art, and a lawyer's time. The lawyer and the art are likely the largest items.
