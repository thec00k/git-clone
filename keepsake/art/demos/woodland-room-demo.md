# Keepsake — Woodland room demo

Three-minute walkthrough of the local prototype. Sample memories only. Narration uses an offline synthetic voice. Friend visits are demonstrated with the local role preview; no online account or multiplayer connection is shown.

## 0:00 — Welcome to Woodland

Welcome to Keepsake. This is the Woodland Writing Room: a quiet place to collect photos, make scrapbooks, and revisit small memories. Move between the writing desk, reading corner, and memory wall.

## 0:15 — Make yourself at home

Room settings bring the atmosphere together. Choose daylight, dusk, or nighttime, change the season and weather, and control individual lights. Balanced and High graphics are saved preferences for the person using the room.

## 0:30 — A scrapbook of your own

Open the book on your desk to begin. Cover and title lets you name the scrapbook and choose its appearance. Turn through your memories with the page arrows, or use the page overview to jump ahead.

## 0:45 — Bring your photos together

Add photos from your computer or phone, or choose memories already in the filing cabinet. A batch can hold twenty photos, arranged four per page. Folder uploads in the cabinet create a category you can name.

## 1:00 — Make every page personal

Add a handwritten touch with the markers, including blue. Adjust the line thickness beside them, then draw directly on the page. Stickers, captions, and the bottom toolbar help you arrange and refine the composition.

## 1:15 — Your bookshelf, growing with you

Your saved scrapbooks live on the upper shelf with readable titles. Hover over a spine to tilt the book outward, then select it to open. The blank book starts a new scrapbook, which becomes your desk book.

## 1:30 — Print a little memory

The mini photo printer gives a memory a physical moment. Choose a photograph and watch it emerge. Then send the print to a scrapbook, or take it to the map and choose where it belongs.

## 1:45 — Notes from friends

With the owner’s permission, friends can view shared scrapbooks and leave a twenty character sticky note. Notes on and off controls their visibility. New notes await approval. This demonstration uses the local friend preview; online visits are still to come.

## 2:00 — Music and a little color

Music settings let you choose a quiet entrance or a configured music selection. Spotify and SoundCloud have their own setup controls. Choose a CRT screen color to match your mood, and adjust music and room sound levels.

## 2:15 — The drawer mini shop

Open the desk drawer to browse the mini shop. Its four categories hold sticker packs, windowsill decorations, room variants, and creative extras. The prototype uses collectible stamps, with room and item availability shown before you choose.

## 2:30 — Small discoveries and quiet hellos

The guestbook collects a quiet hello from a visitor, with an option to request a copy on the desk. Little discoveries add occasional notes around the room. Their frequency and desk note preference are yours to control.

## 2:45 — Keep your memories safe

Your changes save in this browser. Saving and storage shows the current status and lets you download a room backup. Keep one before changing devices or clearing browser data. That is Woodland: a small room that grows with your memories.
